PROBLEM

Paper workflows created duplicate work and avoidable errors.

Researchers needed to collect structured responses in the field without depending on an active internet connection, then synchronize data later.

CONSTRAINTS

Reliability had to survive real-world field conditions.

  • Intermittent or absent connectivity
  • Cross-platform iOS, Android, and Amazon delivery
  • Customer trust in data capture and synchronization
  • Distributed engineering collaboration

DECISIONS

Prioritize offline-first behavior and iterative delivery.

Focused the product around local mobile capture with later synchronization, expanded platform availability, and maintained approximately monthly releases informed by customer needs and support feedback.

WHAT I OWNED

Product work from customer need through release and enablement.

PRODUCT INSIGHT TODAY

For an offline-first workflow, user trust depends on visible synchronization state. A future iteration would make queued uploads, failures, failure reasons, and retry controls explicit so users can verify that field data is safe.
